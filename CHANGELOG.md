## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-30

### Added

- Native macOS menu-bar panel anchored beneath its status item
- Shared draggable timeline with 15-minute precision and animated return to now
- Local timezone comparison with daylight-saving and fractional-offset support
- Working-hours, night, weekend, and wrapping-up status indicators
- Add, edit, remove, copy, and drag-to-reorder timezone workflows
- Light, dark, and system appearance settings
- Configurable home timezone, 24-hour clock, launch-at-login, and timeline sounds
- Custom application and action iconography
